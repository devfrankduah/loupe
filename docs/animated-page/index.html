Built for one bag

Everything for a week fits in one carry-on: a clamshell main compartment, a padded sleeve for a 16-inch laptop, and two quick pockets on the outside for a passport and a phone.

The hip belt tucks behind the back panel when you don't need it, so the pack slides into overhead bins without catching.

Ballistic nylon and recycled lining

The shell is 840-denier ballistic nylon with a water-resistant coating. The lining is recycled polyester, and every zip has a locking pull.
